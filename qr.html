Subcontractor Site Sign-In

Scan to sign in, submit hazard assessments, equipment certificates, and incident reports.

https://joshperlette9497.github.io/site-signin/
Post this at the site entrance / trailer.
  • First-time subcontractors do a 15-second one-time profile setup (name, company, trade).
  • Returning subcontractors go straight to sign-in — their phone remembers them.
  • Same page handles sign-out, hazard assessments, equipment certificates, and incident reports.

Review sign-ins and submitted forms any time in the same app — tap the Admin tab and sign in with your own account.

If the site address changes, regenerate this code — see README.md for how.
